## Meeting 1: Attitude and Mindset (with the director)

### Slide 1: Attitude
Bound to Fail: "A.K.N.Y", Take rejection personally, MF MF then Lipat!, Dependency, Employment Mindset,
Focus on immediate result, Not active online, Zero Ad Spent, "Inconsistency" Disease, Negative Mindset.

Growth Mindset: Never Stop Learning, SW4 Attitude (Some will buy, Some won't, So what? Someone else is waiting),
Numbers don't lie!, Learn everything about your Business, Invest. CEO of your own, Importance of small steps,
Appreciate what you have, Adapt to the Digital Shift, This business will treat you the way you treat it, Gratitude Attitude.

### Slide 2: The Power of Gratitude Mindset
Scenario -> Positive Reaction = Positive Result
Scenario -> Negative Reaction = Negative Result

### Slide 3: Correcting Expectations
1. The Business Owner Reality (Beyond "Employment"): no one wakes you up or checks attendance; your bank account is the only true "manager".
2. Your Brand is the Business: people buy into the trust and expertise of YOU and our TEAM.
3. Marketing is Your Job, Not Just the Developer's/Team: relying only on company/team leads is the "Dependency" trap.
4. Harvest Seasons Require Sowing Seasons: seeds planted today (Information and Attitude) may bear fruit months later.
5. Consistency Trumps Talent: a consistent agent beats a talented one who works once a week (Gratitude Attitude).
6. Everything Has a Purpose: "No" is the Default; a "No" is data, not personal rejection.
7. "I'll get rich quickly without much effort": Real Estate is a struggle; expect the grind before the harvest.
Disclaimer (speech bubble): "Kapag tinamaan ka sa mga sasabihin ko, wag mo isipin na ikaw yun, isipin mo para yun sa katabi mo."
Expectation: Quick Rich, Easy Money. Reality: Grind, Consistency, Struggle.
