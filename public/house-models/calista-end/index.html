Calista End

Description

The Calista End is the corner unit of the Calista townhouse cluster. Because it stands on the corner, it has a bigger lot than the inner units, with more room for a garden.

Official details: 40 sqm floor area on a typical 60.5 sqm lot, 2 bedrooms, 1 toilet and bath, and a carport. Homes are turned over fully finished with painted walls, vinyl flooring and provision for expansion.

Typical lot area 60.5 sqm. Actual lot areas vary per unit and project.
