Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

PHIRST.HOMES is a sales website operated by Team M-BIGTIME, an independent accredited sales team for PHirst Park Homes. It is not the official corporate website of PHirst Park Homes, Inc. This notice explains how we handle personal information you share with us through this website.

Information we collect

When you send an inquiry, we collect the details you choose to provide, such as your full name, mobile number, email address, preferred location, preferred house model and your message.

How we use your information

We use your information only to respond to your inquiry, share information about PHirst house models and developments you asked about, and arrange site visits or follow-up conversations with our sales team.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share the details needed to process your home purchase with PHirst Park Homes and its authorized partners, only with your knowledge and when it is part of your purchase process.

Storage and security

Inquiry submissions are received through our website host (Netlify) and delivered to our team by email. We take reasonable measures to protect your information and keep it only as long as needed for the purposes above.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, or withdraw your consent. Contact us at sales@phirst.homes or +63 969 286 0316.

This notice is provided for information and may be updated. It has not yet been reviewed by legal counsel.
